# Taskify — User Guide

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **Taskify**, a simple and visual task management web application. Taskify helps you organize your daily to-do items, track ongoing work, and monitor your progress across four built-in project groups—all directly in your web browser with no account or login required.

Your tasks are automatically saved in your browser's local storage on the device you are using, so your list remains available when you refresh the page.

---

## 2. First-Time App Access

When you open Taskify for the first time in a browser, you will see a welcome screen introducing the application. Five sample tasks are preloaded automatically so you can explore how the app works right away.

> **[Screenshot Placeholder: Welcome / Onboarding Screen]**  
> *Description: Centered card showing the Taskify checklist illustration, the heading "Task Management & To-Do List", a short description, and the purple "Let's Start" button at the bottom.*

### How to Get Started

1. Open Taskify in your desktop or mobile web browser.
2. Read the welcome message on the **Task Management & To-Do List** screen.
3. Click or tap **Let's Start**.

**Expected Result:** The welcome screen closes and you are taken directly to the **Dashboard**. On future visits in the same browser, Taskify skips the welcome screen and opens the Dashboard automatically.

---

## 3. Understanding the Dashboard

The **Dashboard** is your home screen in Taskify. It gives you a quick summary of your daily progress, active tasks, and task groups.

> **[Screenshot Placeholder: Taskify Dashboard]**  
> *Description: Top greeting ("Hello! Welcome Back") with a `+` quick-add button, followed by the purple Hero Progress Card with a circular percentage ring and "View Task" button, a horizontal "In Progress" card row, and the "Task Groups" list below.*

The Dashboard contains four main areas:

- **Header**: Displays a greeting (**Hello! Welcome Back**) and a **`+`** button on the right to quickly create a new task.
- **Hero Progress Card**: The purple banner at the top showing how many of today's tasks (or total tasks, if none are due today) are completed, along with a circular progress ring and a **View Task** button.
- **In Progress Section**: Displays cards for all tasks currently set to **In Progress**, along with a count badge. Click or tap any card to edit that task.
- **Task Groups Section**: Lists the four built-in groups (**Office Project**, **Personal Project**, **Daily Study**, and **General**) with the number of tasks and completion percentage for each group.

## 4. Creating a New Task

You can add a new task from anywhere in the app.

> **[Screenshot Placeholder: Add New Task Modal]**  
> *Description: Modal form titled "Add New Task" displaying fields for Task Title, Due Date (with Today, Tomorrow, and No Date chips), Task Group dropdown, Status dropdown, Description box, and the purple "Add Task" button.*

### Steps to Create a Task

1. Open the **Add New Task** form using any of these options:
   - Tap the center **`+`** button in the mobile bottom bar.
   - Click **Add New Task** in the desktop left sidebar.
   - Click or tap the **`+`** icon in the top-right header of the Dashboard or Today's Tasks screen.
2. In the **Task Title** field, type a clear name for your task. *(Required)*
3. *(Optional)* Choose a **Due Date**:
   - Click or tap the date field to pick a calendar date, **or**
   - Tap one of the quick chips below the field: **Today**, **Tomorrow**, or **No Date**.
4. In the **Task Group** dropdown, select a group (**Office Project**, **Personal Project**, **Daily Study**, or **General**). The default is **Office Project**.
5. In the **Status** dropdown, select **To-do**, **In Progress**, or **Completed**. The default is **To-do**.
6. *(Optional)* Enter extra notes in the **Description** field.
7. Click or tap **Add Task**.

**Expected Result:** The modal closes, your new task is saved to your browser, and the Dashboard progress counters and task lists update immediately. If you leave **Task Title** empty and submit, an error message (`Please enter a task title.`) appears below the field.

## 5. Editing an Existing Task

You can update a task's title, due date, group, status, or description at any time.

> **[Screenshot Placeholder: Edit Task Modal]**  
> *Description: Modal titled "Edit Task" pre-filled with an existing task's details and a purple "Save Changes" button at the bottom.*

### Steps to Edit a Task

1. Locate the task you want to change:
   - On the **Dashboard**, click or tap any card in the **In Progress** section, **or**
   - On the **Today's Tasks** screen, click or tap the **pencil (Edit)** icon on the bottom-right of the task card.
2. Update any of the fields (**Task Title**, **Due Date**, **Task Group**, **Status**, or **Description**).
3. Click or tap **Save Changes**.

**Expected Result:** The modal closes and your updated task details are saved and displayed immediately.

---

## 6. Completing and Reopening Tasks

You can mark a task as completed or move a completed task back to active status in one tap.

> **[Screenshot Placeholder: Task Card Checkbox]**  
> *Description: A task card on the Today's Tasks screen showing the square checkbox on the left of the title, the "Done" status badge, and strikethrough text for a completed task.*

### Steps to Complete a Task

1. Go to the **Today's Tasks** screen.
2. Find the task you finished.
3. Click or tap the square **checkbox** to the left of the task title.

**Expected Result:** The checkbox fills with a purple checkmark, the task title displays with a strikethrough, its status badge changes to **Done**, and all progress rings update automatically.

### Steps to Reopen a Completed Task

1. Find the completed task on the **Today's Tasks** screen.
2. Click or tap the filled purple **checkbox** next to the task title.

**Expected Result:** The checkmark and strikethrough are removed, and the task's status returns to **To-do**.

---

## 7. Deleting Tasks

If you no longer need a task, you can remove it from your list.

> **Note:** Deleting a task takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.

### Steps to Delete a Task

1. Go to the **Today's Tasks** screen.
2. Find the task you want to remove.
3. Click or tap the **trash can (Delete)** icon in the bottom-right corner of the task card.

**Expected Result:** The task is immediately removed from your list, saved storage, and all progress calculations.

---

## 8. Using Task Groups

Taskify organizes your work into four built-in groups:

- **Office Project**
- **Personal Project**
- **Daily Study**
- **General**

### Steps to View Tasks by Group

1. On the **Dashboard**, scroll to the **Task Groups** section.
2. Click or tap any group card (for example, **Office Project**).

**Expected Result:** Taskify opens the **Today's Tasks** screen filtered to show only tasks in that group. The selected group name appears below the **Today's Tasks** heading with an **`X`** button next to it.

### Steps to Clear the Group Filter

1. On the **Today's Tasks** screen, locate the active group name beneath the heading.
2. Click or tap the **`X`** button next to the group name.

**Expected Result:** The group filter is cleared and tasks from all groups are shown again.

---

## 9. Filtering Tasks by Date

At the top of the **Today's Tasks** screen, a horizontal date strip lets you focus on tasks due on a specific day or view all tasks regardless of date.

> **[Screenshot Placeholder: Date Strip]**  
> *Description: Horizontal row of date pills starting with "Show All Dates", followed by yesterday, Today, and the next four days, with the active pill highlighted in purple.*

### Steps to Filter by Date

1. Open the **Today's Tasks** screen.
2. Click or tap any day pill in the horizontal date strip (for example, **Today** or **Tomorrow**).
3. To see all tasks again—including tasks with **No due date** or dates outside the 6-day strip—click or tap the **Show All Dates** pill on the far left of the strip.

**Expected Result:** The selected pill turns purple, and only tasks matching that due date (or all tasks when **Show All Dates** is selected) are displayed.

---

## 10. Filtering Tasks by Status

Below the date strip on the **Today's Tasks** screen, status pills let you filter tasks by their current stage.

### Steps to Filter by Status

1. Open the **Today's Tasks** screen.
2. Click or tap one of the status pills:
   - **All**: Shows tasks in every status.
   - **To do**: Shows only tasks marked **To-do**.
   - **In Progress**: Shows only tasks marked **In Progress**.
   - **Completed**: Shows only completed tasks.

**Expected Result:** The selected pill turns purple and the task list updates immediately to show only matching tasks.

---

## 11. Searching Tasks

You can search for tasks by keyword on the **Today's Tasks** screen.

### Steps to Search Tasks

1. Open the **Today's Tasks** screen.
2. Click or tap inside the **Search tasks...** box next to the status pills.
3. Type a word or phrase from the task's title or description.

**Expected Result:** The task list filters in real time to show only tasks whose title or description contains your search text (matching any active date, status, or group filters). Clear the search box to show all matching tasks again.

---

## 12. Understanding Progress Indicators

Taskify uses visual indicators on the **Dashboard** and **Today's Tasks** screens to help you track your work:

- **Hero Progress Ring (Dashboard)**: Shows the percentage of completed tasks out of today's tasks. If no tasks are due today, it calculates the percentage across all of your tasks.
- **Hero Status Message**: Updates automatically based on your completion percentage (`Keep going!`, `Your today's task almost done!`, or `Awesome! All your tasks are completed!`).
- **Task Group Rings (Dashboard)**: Each group card displays a circular ring showing the percentage of completed tasks within that specific group.
- **In Progress Bar (Dashboard)**: Each card in the **In Progress** section displays a colored progress bar indicating active work.
- **Overdue Label (Today's Tasks)**: Any incomplete task with a due date earlier than today displays its date in red with **`(Overdue)`** next to it.

## 13. Mobile Navigation

On mobile phones and narrow browser windows (screen width under `768px`), Taskify uses a touch-friendly bottom navigation bar.

> **[Screenshot Placeholder: Mobile Bottom Navigation Bar]**  
> *Description: Rounded light-lavender bottom bar with "Home" on the left, a raised circular purple `+` button in the center, and "Tasks" on the right.*

- **Home**: Opens the **Dashboard** and clears any active group filter.
- **Center `+` Button**: Opens the **Add New Task** modal from any screen.
- **Tasks**: Opens the **Today's Tasks** screen.
- **Back Arrow (`<-`)**: Appears at the top-left of **Today's Tasks** to return to the **Dashboard**.

## 14. Desktop Navigation

On desktop computers and tablets (screen width `768px` and wider), Taskify displays a sticky left sidebar alongside a multi-column main workspace.

> **[Screenshot Placeholder: Desktop Sidebar and Workspace]**  
> *Description: Left sidebar showing the Taskify logo, total completed/total task counter, "Dashboard" and "Today's Tasks" navigation buttons, and the "Add New Task" button, with the main content area on the right.*
